FalconX Whitepaper

Version 1.0 — Project under development

Important: FalconX is currently a project under development. The information in this document describes the proposed design and should not be treated as a guarantee of future performance.

1. Executive Summary

FalconX is a proposed blockchain payment project designed to explore simpler and more accessible digital payments using blockchain technology.

2. Problem Statement

Digital payments can involve complexity, delays, fees and difficulties for users who are new to blockchain technology. FalconX aims to research ways of making blockchain-based payments easier to understand and use.
